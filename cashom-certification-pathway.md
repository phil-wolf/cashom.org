## Curriculum Detail — Certified Server of Cannabis (Tier 1)

Structured by depth level:

- **Brief:** Reciprocity, History, Plant Medicine, Ethics, Lens Framework (preview of Tier 2)
- **Mid:** Cleanliness, Risk Mitigation, Flower Anatomy, Entourage Effect/Terpenes/Cannabinoids, Different Bodies & Different Minds, Guide Method
- **Extensive:** Laws, How to Use in a Hospitality Setting, Consumable Basics

**Essentials variant (proposed):** A lighter slice of Server for lounge staff who don't run their own events — skips the Guide Method, focuses on risk mitigation.

## Tier 2 — Certified Steward of Cannabis

Learning outcome centers on stewarding the experience (not just serving it) — includes holding space for guests while they're high, full GUIDE application, Connoisseurship intro, and deepened Lens Framework work. Natural branch point for specialty certs.

## Tier 3 — CashoM

Mastery tier: advanced Lens Framework work, Connoisseurship, terroir.
